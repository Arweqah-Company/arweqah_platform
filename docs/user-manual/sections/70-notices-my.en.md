## My notices

Every notice and policy that reached you from human resources and corporate communications is here. A notice appears on its publishing day; a policy stays in the «Waiting for my acknowledgement» tab until you acknowledge it, and no extra reminder is sent. The sidebar shows a yellow count of what waits for you, and the home page has number tiles and the pinned notice.

![My notices](../images/en/32-my-notices.png)

### Who sees it

- **View permission:** `notices.view` at scope «own»: you see only what reached you. A notice that did not reach you is shown as «not found».
- **Default roles:** the Employee role holds view, acknowledge and export of your list.
- No platform admin mode is needed.

### What you can do

| Action | What happens | Permission |
|---|---|---|
| Read a notice | Click the row or the eye icon to open the reading window | `notices.view` |
| Acknowledge a policy | Tick «I have read the policy…» after reaching the end of the text, then «Acknowledge» | `notices.acknowledge` |
| Export my list | The rows on screen or all results, with the columns you choose | `notices.export_mine` |

There is no import: a notice is written and approved, not made from a file. A session «signed in as an employee» can read but never acknowledges for the employee.

### How to

**Acknowledge a policy**

1. Open the **Waiting for my acknowledgement** tab and click **Read and acknowledge**.
2. Read to the end; the box stays off with «Read the notice first» under it until the last of the text comes into view.
3. Tick the box and click **Acknowledge**. «Your acknowledgement is recorded. Thank you.» appears and the policy moves to «Acknowledged». **Later** closes the window without acknowledging.

**An announcement:** open it and click **Read**; no acknowledgement is needed.

**A new version:** if the publisher edits a policy you acknowledged, it returns to «Waiting for me» with the badge «Waiting for your acknowledgement of the new version».

**Search and filter:** search by title or text; filter by channel, type, my status, publish date (from and to) and pinned only; switch between table and cards (a notice card shows its type, channel, date, my status and a «Read» or «Read and acknowledge» button). The number waiting for your acknowledgement is a badge on the «Waiting for me» tab.

### Business rules

- The text never changes after publishing; an edit is a new version that goes back for approval, and whoever acknowledged the old one is asked again.
- An archived notice shows «This notice is no longer in force» with no acknowledgement.
- Whoever joins the audience after publishing receives the notice the next night.
- A warning bar under the top bar shows on every page while a notice waits for your acknowledgement, with the count and an «Open my notices» button; it disappears once you acknowledge everything and is not drawn on the notices pages themselves.
